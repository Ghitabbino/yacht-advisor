Yacht Selection

Finding the perfect vessel is not a matter of luck, but of precision.

Every owner is unique. By understanding your needs, we find the tailored solution.

Lifestyle Analysis

We don't just look at models. We analyse how you want to live at sea — family cruising, social events or extreme adventure — reconstructing habits, people on board and expectations. This allows us to identify the hull that fits your rhythm, excluding incoherent options before any comparison.

Technical Verification & Performance

We rigorously assess engine efficiency, hull stability and behaviour, integration of onboard systems and overall build quality. We verify maintenance history, equipment, yard support and value retention to guarantee a reliable, safe vessel as a long-term investment.

Budget Optimisation

We support you on a fiduciary basis in negotiating with brokers and builders, handling surveys, sea trials and contracts. Every detail — equipment, warranties, conditions — is put in writing before signing, so the investment value mirrors your expectations without surprises.
