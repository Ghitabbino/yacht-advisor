Premise

Purchasing a yacht is a significant investment, with a wide range of vessels available on the market, each characterised by specific construction features and intended use.

Prospective owners often lack the expertise and insight required to make a choice that truly fits their lifestyle and expectations at sea.

Crew management can also be complex: the wrong choice or inefficient management can significantly diminish the pleasure of yacht ownership.

Our mission is your complete satisfaction: we work as a single integrated team alongside you — global network, independent fiduciary mandate and absolute discretion — wherever you choose to cruise.

Services

Yacht Selection

Comparative analysis of models and shipyards against your programme of use, with technical due diligence and assessment of long-term value retention.

Crew Management

Assessment of profiles, references and operational fit, with service standards and contractual management to assemble the right team.

Sales Advisory

Market analysis, channel selection and negotiation management with full documentation and discretion to maximise value.

Charter Management

Strategy, distribution and operating processes to make charter use profitable while protecting standards and the asset.

Method

Our operating approach is founded on an in-depth analysis of our clients’ needs, expectations and requirements — through dedicated interviews, active listening and reconstruction of your cruising programme — we define objective, measurable and shared criteria.

We do not propose solutions before thoroughly understanding all aspects: every relevant detail, from layout to time management on board, is considered because nothing is recommended without understanding what serene, surprise-free cruising truly means to you.

The same rigour is applied to all our other services — yacht selection, crew management, sales advisory and charter management — with a consistent method, alternatives systematically compared and a choice that remains defensible over time.

We understand needs, we verify options, we deliver the tailored solution.
